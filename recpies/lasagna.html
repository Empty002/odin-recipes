Lasagna


The recipe

This lasagna recipe is a classic Italian comfort dish made with layers of rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, gooey mozzarella, and tender pasta sheets. It’s hearty, flavorful, and perfect for family dinners or special occasions. The combination of slow-simmered sauce, cheesy filling, and golden baked topping makes it a timeless favorite.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef (or a mix of beef and pork)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh parsley for garnish (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F)
  2. Cook 500g ground beef with 1 chopped onion and 2 cloves garlic until browned
  3. Add 800g canned tomatoes, 2 tbsp tomato paste, salt, pepper, and Italian herbs. Simmer 20 minutes
  4. In another bowl, mix 500g ricotta, 1 egg, and 100g grated Parmesan. In a baking dish, layer meat sauce, lasagna sheets, ricotta mixture, and 200g mozzarella cheese
  5. Repeat layers, finishing with sauce and mozzarella on top
  6. Cover with foil and bake 25 minutes. Remove foil, bake 20 more minutes until golden and bubbling. Let rest 10 minutes before serving
